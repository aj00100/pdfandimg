## 3. Pre-Committed Decision Rules & Leading Indicators

> [!NOTE]
> **Confound & Metric Normalization Notice:**  
> 1. **Seasonality Confound:** Track A is evergreen; Track B demand peaks during application windows and dips post-deadline. This is treated as an acknowledged bias in the readout. Perennial OTR patterns are prioritized to preserve demand beyond single exam deadlines.  
> 2. **Cross-Track Metric Normalization:** Raw impression counts are **not** compared to declare a cross-track winner, because baseline search volumes differ fundamentally between global visa terms and regional Indian exam terms. The test is judged on **Average Position**, **Indexing Velocity**, and **Query Breadth (Count of Ranking Queries)**. Clicks are excluded entirely.

### Week 4 Interim Checkpoint (December 16, 2026)
* **Leading Indicators Evaluated:**
  1. **Indexation Velocity:** URL Inspection API confirms all 6 split-test URLs are "Submitted and indexed" within 7–14 days.
  2. **Query Discovery (Query Breadth):** Each track acquires $\ge 3$ distinct ranking queries in GSC.
  3. **Positional Penetration:** Any query entering Google Top 50 (Positions 20–50).
  4. **Authority Inflow:** At least 2 referring domain placements live and verified.
  5. **Bing Indexation Audit:** Inspect Bing Webmaster API (`InIndex` and `GetPageStats`) to verify indexed page count 4 weeks post-sitemap submission (verifying indexation now that Cloudflare edge logs confirmed 0 Bingbot blocks).
* **Pre-Committed Operational Actions at Week 4:**
  * *If an article is Crawled-Not-Indexed:* Inspect internal link placement on Tools Hub and footer; run immediate mobile-friendly test.
  * *If an article discovers 0 queries:* Re-verify title tag against exact user search intent strings discovered via Google Autocomplete.
  * *If Bing index remains 0:* Check Bing Webmaster UI Site Explorer / URL Inspection to determine whether Bing is filtering pages or merely lagging.

### Week 8 Final Split-Test Checkpoint (January 13, 2027)
* **Comparative Indicators Evaluated:**
  1. **Average Position:** Compare average rank across all ranking queries for Track A vs. Track B.
  2. **Top 30 Penetration:** Total count of queries ranking in positions 11–30.
  3. **Query Breadth:** Total distinct keyword impressions registered per track.
  4. **Backlink Resilience:** Confirm referring domains count ($\ge 5$ quality domains).
* **Pre-Committed Strategic Decision Matrix:**
  * **Scenario 1: Track A (Tier-1 Visa) Wins on Position & Top-30 Penetration:**  
    *Verdict:* High-RPM international search intent can penetrate SERPs even with low initial domain authority.  
    *Action:* Treat this as a tie-breaker. Shift **~50% maximum** of the next batch of 6 content assets toward international visa utilities. Require replication on Batch 2 before executing any broader portfolio reallocation.
  * **Scenario 2: Track B (Indian Exams) Wins on Position & Top-30 Penetration:**  
    *Verdict:* Urgency and acute long-tail queries in India establish rankings faster despite the seasonality bias.  
    *Action:* Treat this as a tie-breaker. Shift **~50% maximum** of the next batch toward recurring Indian exam rejection guides and formatters. Require replication on Batch 2.
  * **Scenario 3: Both Tracks Show Identical Modest Movement (Positions 40–70, query breadth expanding, 0 clicks):**  
    *Verdict:* **Consistent with domain authority bottleneck rather than content defect.**  
    *Action:* **DO NOT rewrite or delete content.** Maintain a steady publishing cadence of 1 guide/week and allocate 70% of effort to acquiring external editorial/educational backlinks.
  * **Scenario 4: Neither Track Registers Queries or Movement (URLs stuck >90 or unindexed):**  
    *Verdict:* Technical indexation suppression or search intent mismatch.  
    *Action:* Conduct a full SERP competitive audit; inspect snippet absorption and re-evaluate backlink placement vector.
