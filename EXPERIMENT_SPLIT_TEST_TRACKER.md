## 3. Pre-Committed Decision Rules & Leading Indicators

> [!NOTE]
> On a brand-new or zero-backlink domain, ranking in positions 30–70 produces impressions but **zero clicks**. Therefore, clicks are explicitly excluded from early decision criteria to prevent false-negative panic.

### Week 4 Interim Checkpoint (December 16, 2026)
* **Leading Indicators Evaluated:**
  1. **Indexation Velocity:** URL Inspection API must confirm all 6 split-test URLs are "Submitted and indexed" within 7–14 days of publishing.
  2. **Initial Impression Footprint:** Target $\ge 10$ organic search impressions per track across long-tail query variations in Google Search Console.
  3. **Positional Penetration:** Any query entering Google Top 50 (Positions 20–50).
  4. **Authority Inflow:** At least 2 referring domain placements live and verified.
* **Pre-Committed Operational Actions at Week 4:**
  * *If an article is Crawled-Not-Indexed:* Inspect internal link placement on Tools Hub and footer; run immediate mobile-friendly test.
  * *If an article has 0 impressions:* Re-verify title tag against exact user search intent strings discovered via Google Autocomplete.

### Week 8 Final Split-Test Checkpoint (January 13, 2027)
* **Leading Indicators Evaluated:**
  1. **Aggregate Impression Volume:** Compare total impressions generated by Track A (Tier-1) vs. Track B (Indian Exams).
  2. **Top 30 Penetration:** Count of queries ranking in positions 11–30.
  3. **Backlink Resilience:** Confirm referring domains count ($\ge 5$ quality domains).
* **Pre-Committed Strategic Decision Matrix:**
  * **Scenario 1: Track A (Tier-1 Visa) Dominates Impressions & Ranks Top 30:**  
    *Verdict:* High-RPM international search intent can penetrate SERPs even with low initial domain authority.  
    *Action:* Pivot 80% of upcoming development to international visa utilities and localized country domains.
  * **Scenario 2: Track B (Indian Exams) Dominates Impressions & Ranks Top 30:**  
    *Verdict:* Urgency and acute search volume in India overcome zero-authority barriers faster.  
    *Action:* Double down on the Indian government exam ecosystem; build out dedicated state PSC, defence, and railway formatting tools.
  * **Scenario 3: Both Tracks Show Identical Modest Movement (Positions 40–70, impressions climbing, 0 clicks):**  
    *Verdict:* Expected behavior for a domain with zero referring domains. The content is healthy; domain authority is the bottleneck.  
    *Action:* **DO NOT rewrite or delete content.** Maintain a steady publishing cadence of 1 guide/week and allocate 70% of effort to acquiring genuine editorial/educational backlinks.
  * **Scenario 4: Neither Track Registers Any Impressions (Zero search impressions across all 6 URLs):**  
    *Verdict:* Technical indexation suppression or search intent mismatch.  
    *Action:* Conduct a full SERP competitive audit; check for Google automated snippet cannibalization or SERP feature displacement.
