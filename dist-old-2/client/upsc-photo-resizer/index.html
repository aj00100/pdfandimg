Understanding the UPSC Photo Requirements

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) has some of the strictest photograph requirements of any examination board in India. Using our UPSC Photo Resizer guarantees your image is accepted on the first try.

  • File Size Constraints: Strictly between 20 KB and 300 KB.
  • Pixel Dimensions: A minimum of 350 pixels (Width) x 350 pixels (Height), up to a maximum of 1000x1000 pixels.
  • Visual Rules: Your face must cover 75% of the total space within the photograph.

Mandatory Rule Update: As per recent UPSC notifications, your photograph must include your Name and the Date the photograph was taken prominently displayed at the bottom of the image. The photo must not be older than 10 days from the start of the online application process. Use our tool above to lock the physical pixel dimensions while preserving top image quality.
