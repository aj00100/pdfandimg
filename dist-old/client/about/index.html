PdfAndImg.

PdfAndImg is a comprehensive suite of 44 precision micro-tools. What started as an advanced binary-search image compressor has evolved into a complete, zero-server framework for PDF manipulation, HEIC conversion, and pixel-perfect cropping.

Our mission is to provide fast, secure, and privacy-first tools for everyone. If you have any questions, suggestions, or need support, please contact us.

The Problem

Most image compressors use generic "Quality" sliders. For users filling out forms with strict KB limits, this is inefficient. You don't care about a "70%" setting; you care about staying under a 20KB or 100KB limit.

Binary Search Solution

We developed a unique Smart Image Compressor algorithm. It automatically tests dozens of quality variations in local memory to find the mathematically highest fidelity that fits exactly inside your target file size.

Dual-Mode Engine

Not just for shrinking — our engine can also enhance small images to meet minimum size requirements. By combining high-fidelity upscaling with metadata padding, we ensure your files meet strict portal minimums without quality degradation.

Instant Bulk Processing

Save time by uploading multiple images at once. Our engine processes them in batches locally on your device and outputs a convenient ZIP file containing all your perfectly sized images.

Crop Image Onlineping

Signature and passport photo requirements often demand specific aspect ratios. Our integrated cropping tool provides presets for Square, Portrait, and Wide-Signature formats to ensure you meet dimensional specs before you compress.

Optimized for Portals

Whether it's SSC, UPSC, or a Bank IBPS portal, our tool is tuned for the exact quirks of government upload systems. We handle the metadata and file headers to guarantee 100% acceptance rates.

100% Privacy

By leveraging the modern Browser Canvas API, all processing happens locally on your hardware. Your photos never leave your device, making PdfAndImg the safest choice for ID documents.

PDF Packaging

Need to submit a photo as a document? Our suite seamlessly converts any JPG, PNG, or WEBP directly into perfectly formatted PDF documents right in your browser, maintaining zero-server confidentiality.

PDF Page Remover

Our highly-requested PDF Page Remover tool allows you to securely delete unwanted or confidential pages from any document instantly. Built with Bank-Grade Security to ensure complete privacy.
