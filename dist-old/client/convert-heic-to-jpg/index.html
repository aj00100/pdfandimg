Convert iPhone HEIC Photos to JPG Securely

Apple iPhones save high-quality photos in the HEIC format to save storage space. However, many government portals, job applications, and older software do not accept HEIC files. Our offline converter instantly changes your HEIC photos to standard JPG format without forcing you to upload your personal pictures to a cloud server.

With PdfAndImg's Zero-Server architecture, you can batch convert your files instantly without worrying about privacy leaks or waiting for slow uploads.

How Our Zero-Server Converter Works

  1. 01

    Local Reading

    Your HEIC files are read securely into your computer's RAM. We never transmit data to the cloud.

  2. 02

    WebAssembly Decoding

    Your browser runs an advanced decoding engine locally to translate the HEIC structure into standard pixels.

  3. 03

    Instant JPG Download

    The pixels are immediately compiled into a fresh JPG and downloaded back to your hard drive.
